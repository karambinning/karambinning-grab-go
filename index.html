A small Levantine kitchen on Leicester Road, Loughborough. Two things done well: chickpea falafel, spiced and crisp at the edges; and shawarma — marinated, slow-cooked, carved off the spit and folded into warm flatbread with the sharp green of pickle and parsley.

Order in for delivery, or come by the shop. Either way: food made when you ask for it, handed over hot.

“The best tasting food, delivered to your door.”

What
we serve.

A short menu, on purpose. Built around the two things we make over and over — and have got good at.

№ 01 · Signature

Falafel

Hand-rolled chickpea fritters, spiced with cumin and coriander, fried to order. Soft inside, crisp on the outside — wrapped, boxed, or in a salad.

Levantine. Vegetarian by nature.

№ 02 · Signature

Shawarma

Marinated meat, stacked and slow-cooked on the spit, then carved thin as it’s ordered. Folded into warm flatbread with garlic sauce, pickles and parsley.

A Levantine classic. Slow cook, fast serve.

№ 03 · From the side

Salads & sauces

Garlic sauce, chilli, lemon. Tomato, cucumber, parsley, pickled turnip — the bright, sharp side of every plate.
